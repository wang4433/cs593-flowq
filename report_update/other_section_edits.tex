We implement the policy and gating pipeline and design a controlled study on
Robomimic Lift, Can, and Square. Frozen Flow BC reaches 60\%, 60\% and 15\%
success on the three tasks. In single-seed full-budget runs, steering the frozen
flow through its input noise raises success from 46\% to 92\% on Lift and from
52\% to 88\% on Can, measured against BC on the same 50 evaluation episodes.
On Square, where only 9 of 100 warmup episodes succeed, its critic diverges and
success falls to zero. The planned evaluation examines action-value calibration,
online sample efficiency, and computation cost, with a shared-critic gate
isolating the effect of the proposed scoring rule.

% (4) Section 4, "Training and evaluation settings": add at the end.
% NEW:
The latent-steering runs use 100 BC warmup episodes, $10^5$ online steps with
20 learner updates per step, KL weight $\beta=3$, and 50 paired evaluation
episodes every $10^4$ online steps. Learner updates run in float64 on an RTX~5080
GPU; simulation and all seeded noise stay on the CPU, so paired evaluations
are unchanged by the device.
